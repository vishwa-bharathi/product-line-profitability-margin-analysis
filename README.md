# Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis
Business intelligence and profitability analysis project focused on identifying margin risk, profit concentration, and portfolio performance using Python, pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit.


## Business Problem

Nassau Candy Distributor operates across multiple confectionery product divisions. 
The company historically measured performance primarily through sales volume, which obscured the true profitability of individual product lines.

This project was developed to provide:

- Product-level profitability visibility
- Margin risk detection
- Revenue vs profit contribution analysis
- Portfolio concentration analysis
- Executive-level business intelligence reporting

The objective was to help leadership make data-driven decisions regarding pricing, cost optimization, portfolio rationalization, and operational strategy.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse product-level gross profitability
- Identify low-margin and high-risk products
- Evaluate division-wise financial performance
- Measure profit concentration risk using Pareto analysis
- Build an interactive Streamlit dashboard for stakeholders
- Generate executive-style strategic recommendations


## Key Metrics Engineered

The following profitability KPIs were engineered during analysis:

- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin (%)
- Revenue Contribution
- Profit Contribution
- Profit per Unit
- Cost Ratio
- Margin Volatility

## Key Findings

### Extreme Profit Concentration
- 5 products generated over 95% of total company profit
- Indicates significant concentration risk

### Chocolate Division Dominance
- Chocolate division contributed approximately 95% of total profit
- Achieved industry-leading gross margins

### Critical Margin Risk Product
- Kazookles generated high revenue but only 8% gross margin
- 92% cost ratio identified severe cost inefficiency

### Portfolio Dependency Risk
- Business profitability heavily dependent on Wonka Bar product variants
- Supply chain or pricing disruptions could significantly impact profitability

## Strategic Recommendations

- Initiate immediate cost review for Kazookles
- Protect Wonka Bar supply chain and pricing structure
- Diversify profit concentration across additional products
- Audit underperforming divisions
- Deploy dashboard for operational KPI monitoring
